About Us

Ladakh is a high-altitude region in northern India, known for its stunning landscapes, including mountains, valleys, and lakes. It has a rich cultural heritage influenced by Tibetan traditions and is recognized for its unique geography and sparse population. Geography and Climate Mountain Ranges: Ladakh is characterized by the Great Himalayas, Zanskar Range, and Karakoram Range, featuring some of the world's highest peaks and passes, including Umling La, the highest drivable road at 19,300 feet (5,882 meters). Climate: The region experiences a cold and dry climate, with average annual precipitation around 3 inches (75 mm). Recent climate change has led to unpredictable weather patterns, including increased temperatures and flash floods.

Our Mission

Ladakh's mission, particularly in the context of recent initiatives, focuses on becoming a hub for sustainable development and scientific research. This includes conducting analog space missions to simulate interplanetary habitats, aiming to prepare for future human habitation on Mars and the Moon, while also promoting economic growth and improved living standards for its residents.Space Exploration Initiatives
