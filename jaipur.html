About Us

Jaipur, the capital city of the Indian state of Rajasthan, is often referred to as the "Pink City" due to the distinct color of its buildings, which were painted pink in 1876 to welcome the Prince of Wales. Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is known for its rich history, stunning architecture, and vibrant culture. Here are some key highlights about the city:
Historical Significance -Founding: Jaipur was one of the earliest planned cities in modern India, designed according to Vastu Shastra (the traditional Indian system of architecture). - Royal Heritage: The city served as the capital of the Kachwaha Rajputs and is dotted with palaces, forts, and temples that reflect its royal past. Major Attractions - Amber Fort: A UNESCO World Heritage Site, this majestic fort is known for its stunning architecture and panoramic views of the surrounding hills. - City Palace: A beautiful blend of Mughal and Rajput architecture, the City Palace houses museums and courtyards that showcase royal artifacts.

Our Mission

aipur, as a city, does not have a singular "mission" statement like a corporation or organization might. However, the vision and mission for the development and governance of Jaipur can be inferred from various initiatives and plans put forth by the local government and urban development authorities. Here are some key aspects that reflect the mission and vision for Jaipur:
1. Sustainable Urban Development Smart City Initiatives: Jaipur has been selected as a Smart City under the Smart Cities Mission by the Government of India, focusing on sustainable and inclusive urban development. The mission aims to enhance the quality of life for residents through improved infrastructure, services, and technology.
2. Cultural Preservation Heritage Conservation: Efforts are made to preserve and promote the rich cultural heritage and historical significance of the city. This includes maintaining and restoring historical sites, monuments, and traditional crafts.
