About Us

Taj Hotels is a renowned chain of luxury hotels and a subsidiary of the Indian Hotels Company Limited, established in 1903. Known for its exceptional hospitality and rich cultural heritage, the brand offers a unique blend of tradition and modern luxury across its properties worldwide.

Our Mission

Taj Hotels aims to create and manage a portfolio of hospitality brands that deliver exceptional experiences, guided by core values of trust, awareness, and joy. Their mission emphasizes excellence in hospitality while fostering community and social responsibility. **Key Aspects of Taj Hotel's Mission** **Excellence in Hospitality**: The mission focuses on providing outstanding service and experiences to guests, ensuring that every stay is memorable. **International Growth**: Taj Hotels aims to expand its presence globally, reaching new markets while maintaining its high standards of service. **Domestic Market Share**: The brand seeks to increase its footprint within India, catering to both domestic and international travelers. **Value Creation for Stakeholders**: The mission includes creating value not just for guests but also for employees, investors, and the communities in which they operate. **Core Values**: Trust, awareness, and joy are central to their operations, guiding every action and decision made within the organization. **Community Focus**: The brand places a strong emphasis on social responsibility, ensuring that community welfare is at the heart of their business practices.
